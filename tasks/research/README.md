# Research

Discovery only. **Does not automatically enter `queued/` or `active/`.**

To promote findings:

1. Summarize in a short note here or `decisions/`.
2. Explicitly add or update a task in `queued/` with Why Now + scope.
3. Never skip the task template.

Anti-pattern: research doc that reads like a roadmap without a decision record.

FILE OUT OSS review (planning only, not a gate): `gate_file_out/GATE_FILE_OUT_OSS_REVIEW.md`. Does not replace P1. Do not implement.

Engineering maturity / execution foundation audit (planning only): `gate_engineering_maturity/GATE_ENGINEERING_MATURITY_AUDIT.md`. Does not replace P1. Do not implement.

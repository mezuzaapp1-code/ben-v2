# Decision 004 — Governance Is Structure, Not Bureaucracy

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-05-24

## Decision

Governance in BEN means **lightweight structure**: append-only lineage, explicit human authority, advisory council — not workflow bureaucracy, approval graphs, or model-based authority.

## Context

Organizations need auditability without another enterprise workflow product. Hats are metadata only. Ledger records proof; it does not execute or gate chat.

## Consequences

- No gates on `/chat` or `/council` from ledger or BEN Log in v1.
- Hats hidden (P6); governance layer (P7) after usage validation.
- Provider tendencies must never become routing or approval authority.
